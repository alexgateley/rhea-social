Prime cost for restaurant owners and franchise operators: food plus labor as a share of sales.
COGS $44,100 + fully loaded labor $44,800 = $88,900 prime cost.
On $140,000 of net sales that is 63.5%.
Rule of thumb: keep it under 60%. The gap here is $4,900 a month.
