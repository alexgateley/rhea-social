Break-even point for restaurant and coffee shop owners: the sales per day where a store starts earning.
Fixed costs $38,500 ÷ 38% contribution margin = $101,316 a month to break even.
That is $3,377 a day. At $4,667 a day, the store breaks even on day 22.
After that, 38¢ of every sales dollar is profit.
