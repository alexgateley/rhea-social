Tip accounting for restaurant, coffee shop and salon owners: tips are a payable, not sales.
$9,000 in card tips is $0 of revenue: it is owed to the team.
What it costs you: 7.65% employer tax ($688.50) and the 2.6% card fee ($234.00), before any FICA tip credit.
That is $922.50 to pass $9,000 through. The FICA tip credit (Form 8846) may cut it: ask your CPA whether you qualify.
