Costco's co-founder on the $1.50 hot dog: don't touch it.
In 2018 Costco's then-CEO Craig Jelinek recalled telling co-founder Jim Sinegal the hot dog combo was losing money; Sinegal's answer, as Jelinek told it: "If you raise the effing hot dog, I will kill you. Figure it out." Costco built its own hot dog plants, and Jelinek said the item then earned a fair return. In 2022 the CFO said the price would hold "a little longer, forever." That's a low price held on purpose. An item whose costs crept past its price without anyone deciding is just a loss.
Reported by Fox Business (Sep 2020, Sep 2022, Feb 2024, citing 425 Business and the Puget Sound Business Journal) and the Washington Times (Sep 24, 2022). Example café numbers are ours, not Costco's.
