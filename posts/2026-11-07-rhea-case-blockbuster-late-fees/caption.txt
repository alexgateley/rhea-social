Blockbuster's hated late fee: nearly $800 million in one year.
The AP reported that late fees brought Blockbuster nearly $800 million in 2000, about 16% of revenue. In December 2004 it announced the end of late fees at its U.S. company stores; its own annual report estimated they would have brought in $400-450 million of revenue in 2005. In the first quarter of 2005, late-fee revenue fell from $175.1 million to $29.8 million, regular rentals rose $99.6 million, and the launch cost about $50 million. Many things led to its 2010 bankruptcy; the lesson here is one: revenue your customers hate is fragile.
Reported by Blockbuster's 2004 Form 10-K and Q1 2005 Form 10-Q (SEC) and the Associated Press (Sept 2010).
