Item 19 can show what stores earn. It doesn't have to.

Under the FTC's Franchise Rule, a franchisor gives you its Franchise Disclosure Document at least 14 calendar days before you sign or pay. Any claim about sales, income or profits has to be in Item 19, and making one is optional, the FTC says. If a franchisor makes one, it needs a reasonable basis and written substantiation, which you can ask to see. For past results it must say which stores, which dates, and how many reached the number, and warn that your results may differ.

Example numbers, ours: an Item 19 shows average sales of $1,000,000 across 100 stores, and 42 of them reached it. That is sales. After food, labor, royalty, rent and everything else, this example store keeps about $100,000, before the loan and the owner's pay.

Ask what the number is: sales or profit, all stores or some. Then call current and former franchisees; the FDD lists them.

Sources: FTC consumer alert, Oct 19, 2026; 16 CFR Part 436.
