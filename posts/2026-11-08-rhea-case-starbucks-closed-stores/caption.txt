Feb 26, 2008: Starbucks closed 7,100 stores. On purpose.
On a Tuesday evening in 2008, Starbucks closed its U.S. company-operated stores for about three to three and a half hours (reports vary) so about 135,000 employees could retrain on espresso. Howard Schultz had returned as CEO the month before. Business Insider and a 2011 TD magazine review cited his book putting the cost at about $6 million, roughly $845 a store. A closed evening shows up on the till; inconsistency never does.
Reported by CNNMoney, NPR, UPI and The Vindicator (Feb 2008), Marketplace (Feb 2008), TD magazine (May 2011) and Business Insider. Example café numbers are ours, not Starbucks'.
