18,000 counterfeit wine labels, found in one wine dealer's home.

Rudy Kurniawan sold rare wine at auction and to wealthy collectors. In 2008, 22 lots of Burgundy said to be from Domaine Ponsot were pulled from a New York auction after the domaine's owner raised doubts. In 2012, agents searching his California home found bottles, corks, stamps and 18,000 counterfeit labels (Wine Spectator; NPR). In December 2013 a federal jury convicted him of mail fraud and wire fraud; in August 2014 he was sentenced to 10 years and ordered to pay $28.4 million in restitution (U.S. Attorney, S.D.N.Y.).

A bottle is worth what its records can prove: where it came from, when, and how many.
