Maria's made $40,000 profit last year. She almost missed payroll.
In one example week her shop made about $770 of profit and still came up $2,150 short on Friday: rent due on the 1st, holiday stock bought ahead, the weekend's card sales still on their way, and a two-week payroll. Profit counts what you earned; cash is what's in the bank today. Once a week, list the next four weeks of cash in and out, rent and payroll first.
Maria's is a pretend coffee shop. Every number is an example.
