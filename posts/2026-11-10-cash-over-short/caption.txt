Cash over and short for restaurant, coffee shop and salon owners: how much drawer variance is normal.
Cash sales 18% × $140,000 = $25,200 a month, $840 a day.
0.3% of that is $2.52 a day, $75.60 a month.
Rule of thumb: that much, drifting over and short, is counting noise. One shift always short is not.
