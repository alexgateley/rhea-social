Subway brought back the $5 footlong. Hundreds of its owners pushed back.

In December 2017 Subway said a $4.99 footlong would return in January. More than 400 franchisees signed a petition against it, the New York Post reported; a January 2018 report in The Denver Post described a petition with nearly 900 signers in 39 states who said they owned Subways. One owner said the ingredients cost about $2, but a footlong cost him well over $4 once labor, rent, royalties and card fees were in. Subway said the deal was optional and that most franchisees supported it.

In September 2018 Subway's CEO told USA Today that each franchisee could decide whether to keep selling it, and that value should fit local costs.

Ingredients aren't the cost. All-in is.
