Same $30 bottle: $21 in the tasting room, $6 on a shelf.

Example numbers: $9 of wine in a $30 bottle. In the tasting room the guest pays $30 and the winery keeps all of it: $21 over the wine. On a store shelf, a common rule of thumb: the winery sells to the distributor at about half of retail ($15), the distributor adds about a third ($20 to the store), the store adds about half ($30). The winery's $6 against the room's $21: three and a half times.

The room has its own costs: staff, space, card fees. So count margin by channel, not just bottles.

Rule of thumb from Jeff Morgan, quoted by Joshua E. London ("This Wine Costs What?", Oct 16, 2024); channel margin ranges from Farm Credit East (Mar 17, 2026).
