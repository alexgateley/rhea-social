NPC's stores sold for about $800 million. Its debt at filing was $903 million.

NPC International was the largest franchisee of both Pizza Hut and Wendy's, with about $1.6 billion in sales in 2019. It filed for Chapter 11 in July 2020 and in August agreed with Pizza Hut to close up to 300 of its Pizza Huts, mostly dine-in. In January 2021 the bankruptcy court approved two buyers: Flynn Restaurant Group, for more than 900 Pizza Huts and about half the Wendy's (a reported $553 million), and existing Wendy's franchisees, through a Wendy's-led bid, for the rest (just over $248 million). The buyers agreed to offer jobs to all of NPC's restaurant workers. The sale closed in March 2021; Franchising.com called it the largest franchisee deal in history.

Rough math on the reported figures: about $600,000 a store, roughly half a year of sales. Buyers price the stores, not your debt.

Reported by Restaurant Dive, Nation's Restaurant News, Franchising.com, KOAM and NPC's own releases (2020 to 2021); The Wendy's Company's 10-Q (2021).
