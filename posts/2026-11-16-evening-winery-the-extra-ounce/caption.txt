One extra ounce a glass is about 470 bottles a year.

Example numbers: a $14 glass, from a bottle with $9 of wine in it (a bottle holds 25.4 oz). Pour 5 ounces and a bottle gives about 5 glasses; pour 6 and it's just over 4, so each bottle rings up about $12 less. A room pouring 40 glasses a day, 300 days a year, gives away 12,000 ounces: about 470 bottles, roughly $4,300 of wine, and 2,400 glasses' worth that never hit the register.

Nobody steals it and nobody rings it up. Count it every week: bottles opened against glasses sold.
