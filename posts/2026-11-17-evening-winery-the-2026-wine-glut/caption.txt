Americans bought about 7 million fewer cases of wine in 2025.

Silicon Valley Bank's State of the US Wine Industry report (Jan 15, 2026): about 329 million cases in 2025, down from 335.9 million; about $74.3 billion, down from $75.5 billion. Inventories are high across the whole supply chain. At smaller hospitality-focused wineries, tasting room visits fell and so did average checks. The report's author, Rob McMillan, expects the market to bottom in 2027-2028. Reported coverage also says top performers use targeted price adjustments rather than broad discounting (Vinetur, Jan 15, 2026).

Example numbers: 100 visitors at $80 is $8,000. Ten percent fewer people spending ten percent less: $6,480. Down 19%. Two small drops make one big one, so track visits and the average check separately, every week.
