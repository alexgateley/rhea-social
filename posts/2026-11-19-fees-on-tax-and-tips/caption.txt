Credit card fees on sales tax and tips: a hidden cost for restaurant, coffee shop and salon owners.
$80,000 of card sales carries $6,600 of 8.25% sales tax and $6,000 of tips.
The processor charges 2.9% on all of it: $365.40 a month on money that isn't yours.
That is $4,384.80 a year. On passing tip fees to staff, ask your CPA.
