Sales tax tip for restaurant, coffee shop and salon owners: set collected tax aside every week.
The sales tax in your deposits is owed to the state from the day it's collected.
Move it to a separate account every week, by LLC.
Then the return never meets an empty account. Rates, due dates and rules: ask your CPA.
