Rhea is built in memory of Kathy.

Kathy was a bookkeeper and a single mother of three. When her divorce left her without the money to keep our home, we walked every business park with her, slipping fliers for her bookkeeping services under the doors. She went on to build a very successful business.

All three of her kids grew up and went to school for accounting, following in her footsteps. I ended up doing other things, but Kathy managed the books for my businesses until the day she died, suddenly, from breast cancer.

We lost our mom, and our kids lost their grandma. Our books have never been the same.

Kathy is survived by her two sons, a daughter and four grandchildren. Kathy was most proud to be a grandma.

Rhea is built by her two sons, in memory of Grandma Kathy. With every decision we make, it feels like she’s at the table with us.

— Alex

October is Breast Cancer Awareness Month. In memory of Kathy.
